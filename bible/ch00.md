# How to Use This Bible

## The exam (20188 Quantitative Finance and Derivatives — Module 1)
- **Written exam, 100 points**, mapped to the final grade (out of 31) with the Bocconi Graduate School curve (18–20: 10%, 21–23: 25%, 24–27: 30%, 28–29: 25%, 30–31: 10% of students).
- **Structure (all past exams 2022–2026):**
  - **Theory questions — 20 points:** usually one *open* question (6 pts: "State the 1st/2nd FTAP…", "Define an arbitrage opportunity…", "Define a RN probability…", "State the BS PDE…") plus 2–3 *multiple-choice* questions (4–6 pts each; "mark all appropriate answers": **full marks only if all and only the correct options are marked; any wrong option gives 0**).
  - **Exercise 1 — 40/45 points:** a discrete-time market — either a **one-period trinomial market** (incomplete; RN probabilities in parametric form, NA price intervals, super-replication, completing the market with a derivative) or, in all recent exams, a **two-period tree** with stochastic interest rates (bond prices, RN probabilities, completeness, European/exotic option prices by backward induction, futures/forwards/swaps, buy-and-hold strategies).
  - **Exercise 2 — 35/40 points:** the **Black–Scholes model** (historical vs risk-neutral probabilities, pricing exotic payoffs by decomposition into calls/puts/digitals/power claims, replicating strategies, Itô's formula, conditional expectations at intermediate dates).
- Calculators allowed; results with $N(\cdot)$ may be left in terms of the standard normal cdf.

## Structure of the Bible
- **One chapter per lecture** (slide decks follow the textbook [BOR26] numbering: Ch. 1, 2, 3–4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16; Ch. 6, 12, 13 were not covered).
- Each chapter: **theory** (slides + what was said in class + essential additions), then **past exam questions** (theory questions with the correct answer and the reason, and full exercises with step-by-step solutions), then a slot for **tutorial exercises**.
- **Boxes marked ★ EXAM** contain the items of the **official list "Theory topics for the exam 2026–27"**, with what is required: *definition*, *statement*, *statement only*, *statement and derivation*. Learn these **word by word**.
