**[Jan 14, 2022 – General, Q1 (6 pts)]** *In the one-period market model state the First Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing.*
**Answer.** In a one-period financial market the following are equivalent: (1) the market is arbitrage-free; (2) there exists a risk-neutral probability $Q$ (i.e. $Q[\omega_k]>0$ for all $k$, $\sum_kQ[\omega_k]=1$, $S_j(0)=\mathbb{E}^Q[S_j(1)/(1+r)]$ for $j=1,\dots,N$). (Theorem 27.)

**[Dec 19, 2025, Q1 (6 pts)]** *Define a Risk Neutral Probability in the one-period market model.*
**Answer.** A probability $Q$ on $\Omega=\{\omega_1,\dots,\omega_K\}$ is a risk-neutral probability if (1) $Q[\omega_k]>0$ for all $k$; (2) $\sum_{k=1}^KQ[\omega_k]=1$; (3) $S_j(0)=\mathbb{E}^Q\big[\frac{S_j(1)}{1+r}\big]$ for every risky asset $j=1,\dots,N$ (Definition 20).
