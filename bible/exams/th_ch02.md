#### [Jan 14, 2022 General — Q1] · [Jan 2024 1st exam — Q1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** In the one-period market model state the First Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing.

**Answer.** In a one-period financial market the following are equivalent:
1. no-arbitrage holds;
2. there exists a risk-neutral probability $Q$.

(Theorem 27.)

#### [Dec 19, 2025 — Question 1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** Define a Risk Neutral Probability in the one-period market model.

**Answer.** A probability $Q$ on $\Omega=\{\omega_1,\dots,\omega_K\}$ is a risk-neutral probability if:
1. $Q[\omega_k]>0$ for all $k=1,\dots,K$;
2. $\sum_{k=1}^KQ[\omega_k]=1$;
3. for every risky asset $j=1,\dots,N$: $\displaystyle S_j(0)=\mathbb{E}^Q\Big[\frac{S_j(1)}{1+r}\Big]$.

(Definition 20.)
