#### [Jan 14, 2022 2nd Partial — Question 1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** State the Second Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing in the multiperiod market model.

**Answer.** In a discrete-time multi-period financial market the following are equivalent:
1. the market is both arbitrage-free and dynamically complete;
2. there exists one and only one equivalent martingale measure (risk-neutral probability) $Q$.

(Theorem 122.)

#### [Feb 1, 2023 2nd Partial — Question 3] (4 points, mark the appropriate alternatives)
**Question.** The multiperiod discrete market is dynamically complete and arbitrage free if and only if

- **A.** there exists a unique risk-neutral probability measure
- **B.** there exists at least a risk-neutral probability measure
- **C.** there are no arbitrage opportunities and any cashflow can be dynamically replicated
- **D.** there are no risk-neutral probability measures

**Correct answers: A, C.**
- A is the 2nd FTAP (Theorem 122); C is the definition itself (NA + every claim attainable).
- B characterizes NA only (1st FTAP); D means the market has arbitrage.
