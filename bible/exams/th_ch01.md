**[Jan 13, 2025, Q1 (6 pts)]** *In the one-period market define a violation of the Law of One Price.*
**Answer.** A one-period market permits violations of the LOP if there exist two strategies $\vartheta,\vartheta'$ such that $V_\vartheta(0)\ne V_{\vartheta'}(0)$ but $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)=V_{\vartheta'}(1)(\omega_k)$ for all $k=1,\dots,K$ (Definition 7).

**[Jan 2026, Q1 (6 pts)]** *Define an arbitrage opportunity in the one-period market model.*
**Answer.** The market admits an arbitrage opportunity if there is a strategy $\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{N+1}$ with (1) $V_\vartheta(0)\le0$; (2) $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)\ge0$ for all $k$; and (3) at least one of: $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_{\bar k})>0$ for some $\bar k$, or $V_\vartheta(0)<0$ (Definition 9).
