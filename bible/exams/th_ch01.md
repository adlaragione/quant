#### [Jan 13, 2025 — Question 1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** In the one-period market define a *violation* of the Law of One Price.

**Answer.** A one-period financial market permits violations of the law of one price if there exist two strategies $\vartheta$ and $\vartheta'$ such that
$$V_\vartheta(0)\neq V_{\vartheta'}(0)\qquad\text{but}\qquad V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)=V_{\vartheta'}(1)(\omega_k)\ \text{ for all } k=1,\dots,K.$$
(Definition 7.)

#### [Jan 2026 — Question 1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** Define an arbitrage opportunity in the one-period market model.

**Answer.** A one-period market admits an arbitrage opportunity if there exists a strategy $\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{N+1}$ such that:
1. $V_\vartheta(0)\le0$;
2. $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)\ge0$ for all $k=1,\dots,K$;
3. at least one of the following holds: (I) $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_{\bar k})>0$ for some $\bar k$; or (II) $V_\vartheta(0)<0$.

(Definition 9.)
