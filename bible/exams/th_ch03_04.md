#### [Jan 31, 2022 General — Q1] · [Jan 16, 2023 General — Q1] · [Jan 2024 2nd exam — Q1] · [Jan 29, 2025 — Q1] (6 points, open question)
**Question.** In the one-period market model state the Second Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing.

**Answer.** In a one-period financial market the following statements are equivalent:
1. both no-arbitrage and market completeness hold;
2. there exists one, and only one, risk-neutral probability.

(Theorem 47.)

#### [Dec 19, 2025 — Question 2] (6 points, mark all the appropriate answers)
**Question.** Consider a one-period arbitrage-free market. A non-redundant (i.e. non-replicable) derivative with final payoff $X\in\mathbb{R}^K$ is introduced in this market. Denote by $S_X(0)$ a no-arbitrage price for $X$ at $t=0$. Then:

- **A.** There exists $\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{N+1}$ such that $V_\vartheta(1)=X$.
- **B.** $\displaystyle\max_{\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{N+1}:\,V_\vartheta(1)\ge-X}\big(-V_\vartheta(0)\big)=\sup_Q\mathbb{E}^Q\Big[\frac{X}{1+r}\Big]$, where the sup is taken among all RN probabilities $Q$ of the original market, and $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)\ge-X(\omega_k)$ holds $\forall\omega_k\in\Omega$.
- **C.** $\displaystyle\max_{\vartheta:\,V_\vartheta(1)\ge-X}\big(-V_\vartheta(0)\big)<S_X(0)<\min_{\vartheta:\,V_\vartheta(1)\ge X}V_\vartheta(0)$.
- **D.** $\displaystyle\min_{\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{N+1}:\,V_\vartheta(1)\ge X}V_\vartheta(0)=\sup_Q\mathbb{E}^Q\Big[\frac{X}{1+r}\Big]$, where the sup is taken among all RN probabilities $Q$ of the original market, and $V_\vartheta(1)(\omega_k)\ge X(\omega_k)$ holds $\forall\omega_k\in\Omega$.

**Correct answers: C, D.**
- A is false: that would mean $X$ is replicable (redundant).
- B is false: the maximum amount raised against a liability not exceeding $X$ equals $\inf_Q$, not $\sup_Q$.
- C is Proposition 55 (open interval of NA prices).
- D is the LP duality: the minimum super-replication cost equals $\sup_Q\mathbb{E}^Q[X/(1+r)]$.

#### [Jan 2026 — Question 2] (6 points, mark all the appropriate answers)
**Question.** Consider a one-period financial market with $K$ final scenarios and $1+N$ securities. Denote with $A$ the payoff matrix, with $K$ rows and $1+N$ columns.

- **A.** The market is complete if and only if the rank of $A$ is equal to $N+1$.
- **B.** The market is complete if and only if the rank of $A$ is equal to $K$.
- **C.** The market is complete if $N+1\ge K$.
- **D.** The market is incomplete if $N+1<K$.

**Correct answers: B, D.**
- B is Proposition 38.
- D: $\operatorname{rank}A\le N+1<K$, so the rank can never reach $K$.
- A is false: the rank must equal the number of states, not of securities.
- C is false: enough securities is necessary but not sufficient — they must have linearly independent payoffs.
