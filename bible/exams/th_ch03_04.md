**[Jan 31, 2022 – General, Q1 (6 pts)]; [Jan 16, 2023 – General, Q1]; [Jan 29, 2025, Q1]; [Jan 2024 (2nd exam), Q1]** *In the one-period market model state the Second Fundamental Theorem of Asset Pricing.*
**Answer.** In a one-period financial market the following are equivalent: (1) the market is both arbitrage-free and complete; (2) there exists one, and only one, risk-neutral probability. (Theorem 47.)

**[Dec 19, 2025, Q2 (6 pts)]** *One-period arbitrage-free market; a non-redundant derivative $X\in\mathbb{R}^K$ is introduced, $S_X(0)$ a NA price. Then (mark all):*
A. there exists $\vartheta$ with $V_\vartheta(1)=X$ — B. $\max_{\vartheta:V_\vartheta(1)\le X}V_\vartheta(0)=\sup_Q\mathbb{E}^Q[\frac{X}{1+r}]$ — C. $\max_{\vartheta:V_\vartheta(1)\le X}V_\vartheta(0)<S_X(0)<\min_{\vartheta:V_\vartheta(1)\ge X}V_\vartheta(0)$ — D. $\min_{\vartheta:V_\vartheta(1)\ge X}V_\vartheta(0)=\sup_Q\mathbb{E}^Q[\frac{X}{1+r}]$.
**Answer: C, D.** A contradicts non-redundancy. B is wrong: max sub-replication value $=\inf_Q$, not $\sup_Q$. C is Prop. 55 (the lower bound $\max_{\vartheta \text{ s.r. of }-X}(-V_\vartheta(0))$ equals the max value of portfolios dominated by $X$). D is the LP duality (§4.6).

**[Jan 2026, Q2 (6 pts)]** *One-period market, $K$ scenarios, $1+N$ securities, payoff matrix $A$ ($K\times(1+N)$). Mark all:* A. complete iff $\operatorname{rank}A=N+1$ — B. complete iff $\operatorname{rank}A=K$ — C. complete if $N+1\ge K$ — D. incomplete if $N+1<K$.
**Answer: B, D** (Prop. 38; $\operatorname{rank}A\le N+1<K$ in D). C is false: enough securities is necessary, not sufficient (they must be linearly independent).
