### [Jan 14, 2022 – General] Exercise 1 (45 pts) — trinomial market, incomplete, non-redundant derivative

**Data.** One-period market, riskless $B$ with $r=2\%$, risky $S$ with $S(1)(\omega_1)=15.3$, $S(1)(\omega_2)=5.1$, $S(1)(\omega_3)=10.2$.

1. *(4) Is the market complete?* **No.** $A$ is $3\times2$: $\operatorname{rank}A\le2<3=K$ (fewer securities than states) ⇒ incomplete (Prop. 38).
2. *(6) $S(0)=7.5$. Find $Q(\omega_2),Q(\omega_3)$ in terms of $q_1$.* Solve $\frac{1}{1.02}[15.3q_1+5.1q_2+10.2(1-q_1-q_2)]=7.5$ ⇒ $15.3q_1+5.1q_2+10.2-10.2q_1-10.2q_2=7.65$ ⇒ $5.1q_1-5.1q_2=-2.55$ ⇒ $q_2=q_1+\tfrac12$, $q_3=1-q_1-q_2=\tfrac12-2q_1$. **Answer C:** $Q(\omega_2)=q_1+\frac12$, $Q(\omega_3)=\frac12-2q_1$.
3–4. *(3+3) Bounds on $q_1$.* $q_1>0$, $q_2=q_1+\frac12>0$ (automatic), $q_3=\frac12-2q_1>0$ ⇔ $q_1<\frac14$. ⇒ **$q_1$ strictly larger than 0 and strictly smaller than $0.25$.**
5. *(4) Arbitrage-free?* **Yes**: RN probabilities exist ($q_1\in(0,0.25)$) ⇒ NA by the 1st FTAP.
6. *(7) Derivative on the linear return $R=\frac{S(1)-S(0)}{S(0)}$: $X=1.02$ if $R>0$, $2.04$ otherwise.* Returns: $R(\omega_1)=1.04>0$, $R(\omega_2)=-0.32<0$, $R(\omega_3)=0.36>0$ ⇒ $X=(1.02,\,2.04,\,1.02)$.
   Replicable? $\det\begin{pmatrix}1.02&15.3&1.02\\1.02&5.1&2.04\\1.02&10.2&1.02\end{pmatrix}=5.31\ne0$ ⇒ $\operatorname{rank}[A|X]=3\ne2=\operatorname{rank}A$ ⇒ **NO**, not replicable.
   NA prices: $\frac{\mathbb{E}^Q[X]}{1.02}=q_1+2q_2+q_3=q_1+2q_1+1+\frac12-2q_1=q_1+\frac32$, $q_1\in(0,0.25)$ ⇒ **NA price interval $(1.5,\ 1.75)$** (open).
7. *(4) Super-replicating strategies $\vartheta=(x,y)$:* $1.02x+15.3y\ge1.02$, $1.02x+5.1y\ge2.04$, $1.02x+10.2y\ge1.02$, i.e.
   $$y\ge\tfrac{1-x}{15}\ (\omega_1),\qquad y\ge\tfrac{2-x}{5}\ (\omega_2),\qquad y\ge\tfrac{1-x}{10}\ (\omega_3).$$
   Set $=\{(x,y): y\ge\max\{\frac{1-x}{15},\frac{2-x}{5},\frac{1-x}{10}\}\}$. Comparing the lines: $\omega_2$-line dominates for $x\le2.5$, $\omega_1$-line for $x\ge2.5$ (the $\omega_3$ constraint is never binding: at $x=2.5$ it gives $-0.15<-0.1$). So: $y\ge\frac{2-x}{5}$ if $x\le2.5$; $y\ge\frac{1-x}{15}$ if $x>2.5$.
8–9. *(2+2) Super-replicating strategy with maximum initial cashflow $C_\vartheta(0)=-(x+7.5y)$, i.e. minimum cost.* Iso-cost lines $y=\frac{c-x}{7.5}$ have slope $-\frac{1}{7.5}$, between the slopes of the binding constraints ($-\frac15$ and $-\frac1{15}$) ⇒ optimum at the kink: $\frac{2-x}{5}=\frac{1-x}{15}$ ⇒ $x^*=2.5$, $y^*=-0.1$. Cost $=2.5-0.75=1.75$ = **upper bound** of the NA interval (LP duality, §4.6), as it must be. Check: payoffs $1.02\cdot2.5+(15.3,5.1,10.2)(-0.1)=(1.02,\,2.04,\,1.53)\ge X$ ✓.
   > *Note:* the official solution sheet states $x=3$, $y=-0.2$ with value $1.5$; that point is the intersection of the $\omega_2$ and $\omega_3$ lines but **violates** the $\omega_1$ constraint ($3+15(-0.2)=0<1$), so it is not super-replicating. The value $1.5$ is the *lower* bound (best sub-replication: maximize $x+7.5y$ s.t. payoff $\le X$ ⇒ vertex $\omega_2\cap\omega_3$: $(3,-0.2)$, value $1.5$ — that is the maximal cash raised against a liability $\le X$). Know both: **min super-replication cost $=1.75=\sup_Q$; max sub-replication value $=1.5=\inf_Q$.**
10. *(5) The derivative trades at 1.6.* $1.6\in(1.5,1.75)$ ⇒ extended market **arbitrage-free**; $X$ non-redundant ⇒ adding it raises rank to 3 ⇒ **complete**. **Answer A.**
11. *(5) New derivative $Y=K$ if $R>0$, $2K$ otherwise, price 8. Find $K$.* Unique $Q$ of the extended market: $q_1+1.5=1.6$ ⇒ $q_1=0.1$, $q_2=0.6$, $q_3=0.3$. Then $8=\frac{0.1K+1.2K+0.3K}{1.02}=\frac{1.6K}{1.02}$ ⇒ **$K=5.1$**. (Equivalently $Y=\frac{K}{1.02}X$ ⇒ price $=\frac{K}{1.02}1.6$.)

### [Jan 31, 2022 – General] Exercise 1 (45 pts) — trinomial market, call, super-replication, bull spread

**Data.** $r=5\%$; $S(1)=(12.6,\ 10.5,\ 8.4)$; $S(0)=9.4$.

1. *(3) Complete?* **No**: 3 states, 2 securities ⇒ $\operatorname{rank}A\le2<3$.
2. *(4) $Q$ in terms of $q_3$.* $\frac{12.6q_1+10.5q_2+8.4q_3}{1.05}=9.4$, i.e. $12q_1+10q_2+8q_3=9.4$ with $q_1=1-q_2-q_3$ ⇒ $q_2=1.3-2q_3$, $q_1=q_3-0.3$. **Answer D:** $Q(\omega_1)=-\frac{3}{10}+q_3$, $Q(\omega_2)=\frac{13}{10}-2q_3$.
3–4. *(2+2)* $q_1>0$ ⇔ $q_3>0.3$; $q_2>0$ ⇔ $q_3<0.65$ ⇒ $q_3\in(0.3,\,0.65)$.
5. *(4)* RN probabilities exist ⇒ **arbitrage-free** (1st FTAP).
6. *(6) Call $K_L=9.45$:* payoff $(3.15,\ 1.05,\ 0)$. $\det\begin{pmatrix}1.05&12.6&3.15\\1.05&10.5&1.05\\1.05&8.4&0\end{pmatrix}=2.3153\ne0$ ⇒ **not replicable**. Price $=\frac{3.15(q_3-0.3)+1.05(1.3-2q_3)}{1.05}=q_3+0.4$ ⇒ **NA interval $(0.7,\ 1.05)$**.
7. *(4) Super-replication:* $x+12y\ge3$, $x+10y\ge1$, $x+8y\ge0$ (dividing by 1.05), i.e. $y\ge\max\{\frac{3-x}{12},\frac{1-x}{10},-\frac{x}{8}\}$. The binding envelope is made of the $\omega_1$ line ($x\le-6$) and the $\omega_3$ line ($x>-6$): $\{y\ge\frac{3-x}{12}$ if $x\le-6$; $y\ge-\frac x8$ if $x>-6\}$.
8–9. *(2+2) Minimum-cost super-replicating strategy:* iso-cost lines $x+9.4y=c$; optimum at the kink $\omega_1\cap\omega_3$: $x+12y=3$, $x+8y=0$ ⇒ $y^*=0.75$, $x^*=-6$. Cost $-6+0.75\cdot9.4=1.05=\sup_Q$ ✓.
10. *(3) Call trades at 0.9.* $0.9\in(0.7,1.05)$ and $\operatorname{rank}[A|c_L]=3$ ⇒ **A: arbitrage-free AND complete.** Unique $Q$: $q_3+0.4=0.9$ ⇒ $Q=(0.2,\ 0.3,\ 0.5)$.
11. *(4) Call with strike $K_H\ge9.45$:* for $9.45\le K_H<10.5$: $(12.6-K_H,\ 10.5-K_H,\ 0)$; for $10.5<K_H\le12.6$: $(12.6-K_H,\ 0,\ 0)$.
12. *(4)* $c_{K_H}(0)=\frac{0.2(12.6-K_H)+0.3(10.5-K_H)}{1.05}=5.4-0.4762K_H$ for $9.45\le K_H<10.5$; $=\frac{0.2(12.6-K_H)}{1.05}=2.4-0.19048K_H$ for $10.5<K_H\le12.6$.
13. *(2) Price 0.2:* first branch gives $K_H=10.92\notin[9.45,10.5)$ — rejected; second gives **$K_H=11.55$** ✓.
14. *(3) Bull spread $(S-K_L)^+-(S-K_H)^+$:* by linearity $0.9-0.2=$ **0.7**.

### [Jan 16, 2023 – General] Exercise 1 (40 pts) — two risky assets, redundant security, best-of option, Margrabe

**Data.** $r=5\%$; $S_1(1)=(4.2,\ 3.15,\ 2.1)$, $S_2(1)=(7.35,\ 4.2,\ 1.05)$; $S_1(0)=2.7$, $S_2(0)=3.1$.

1. *(4) Complete?* $A=\begin{pmatrix}1.05&4.2&7.35\\1.05&3.15&4.2\\1.05&2.1&1.05\end{pmatrix}$, $\det A=0$ (indeed $S_2(1)=3S_1(1)-5.25$, i.e. $S_2$ is redundant) ⇒ $\operatorname{rank}A=2<3$ ⇒ **incomplete**.
2. *(7) RN probabilities:* $4q_1+3q_2+2q_3=2.7$ and $7q_1+4q_2+q_3=3.1$ (prices divided by 1.05), $q_1=1-q_2-q_3$ ⇒ $q_2+2q_3=1.3$ and $3q_2+6q_3=3.9$ (the same equation: consistent, so LOP holds). ⇒ $q_2=1.3-2q_3$, $q_1=q_3-0.3$, $q_3\in(0.3,\ 0.65)$.
3. *(3)* Infinitely many RN probabilities ⇒ **arbitrage-free** (1st FTAP).
4. *(7) Best-of option $X=\max\{S_1(1),S_2(1)\}=(7.35,\ 4.2,\ 2.1)$.* $\det[\text{cols }B,S_1,X]=-1.16\ne0$ ⇒ rank 3 ⇒ **not replicable**. Price $=7q_1+4q_2+2q_3=q_3+3.1$ ⇒ **NA interval $(3.4,\ 3.75)$**.
5. *(8) Super-replication with $B$ and $S_1$ only, $\vartheta=(x,y,0)$:* $x+4y\ge7$, $x+3y\ge4$, $x+2y\ge2$ ⇒ $y\ge\max\{\frac{7-x}4,\frac{4-x}3,\frac{2-x}2\}$. Envelope: the $\omega_3$ line for $x\le-3$, the $\omega_1$ line for $x\ge-3$ (the $\omega_2$ constraint is never binding: at $x=-3$ it gives $7/3<2.5$).
6–7. *(2+2) Maximum initial cashflow (= minimum cost $x+2.7y$):* slope $-1/2.7$ lies between $-\frac12$ and $-\frac14$ ⇒ optimum at the kink $\omega_1\cap\omega_3$: $x+4y=7$, $x+2y=2$ ⇒ **$x^*=-3$, $y^*=2.5$**; cost $-3+6.75=3.75=\sup_Q$ ✓ (payoff $(7.35,\,4.725,\,2.1)\ge X$).
   > *Note:* the official sheet reports $(x,y)=(-2,2)$ with value $3.4$, obtained intersecting the $\omega_2$ and $\omega_3$ lines; that portfolio pays $(6.3,4.2,2.1)$ and **fails** to super-replicate in $\omega_1$. $3.4$ is the *lower* NA bound (maximal sub-replication value). The minimum super-replication cost must equal the **upper** bound $3.75$ (§4.6).
8. *(4) Option trades at 3.6* $\in(3.4,3.75)$ ⇒ **A: arbitrage-free AND complete** (the option completes the market).
9. *(3) Margrabe (exchange) option $\max\{S_1(1)-S_2(1),0\}$.* Since $\max\{S_1-S_2,0\}=\max\{S_1,S_2\}-S_2$, its price is $3.6-3.1=$ **0.5**. (Check: unique $Q$ from $q_3+3.1=3.6$: $Q=(0.2,0.3,0.5)$; payoff $(0,0,1.05)$ ⇒ $\frac{1.05\cdot0.5}{1.05}=0.5$.)
