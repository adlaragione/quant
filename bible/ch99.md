# Appendix — Formula Sheet and Exam Recipes

## A.1 One-period market
- $V_\vartheta(0)=\vartheta_0+\sum_j\vartheta_jS_j(0)$, $V_\vartheta(1)=A\vartheta$.
- RN probability: $q_k>0$, $\sum q_k=1$, $S_j(0)=\frac{1}{1+r}\sum_kq_kS_j(1)(\omega_k)$. State prices $\psi_k=q_k/(1+r)$.
- **1st FTAP:** NA ⟺ ∃ $Q$. **2nd FTAP:** NA + complete ⟺ ∃! $Q$. **Completeness:** $\operatorname{rank}A=K$.
- Redundant $X$: $S_X(0)=V_{\vartheta_X}(0)=\mathbb{E}^Q[X]/(1+r)$. Non-redundant: $\inf_Q<S_X(0)<\sup_Q$ (open); $\min$ super-replication cost $=\sup_Q$; $\max$ sub-replication value $=\inf_Q$.
- Binomial: NA ⟺ $d<1+r<u$; $q_u=\frac{1+r-d}{u-d}$; $\Delta=\frac{X_u-X_d}{(u-d)S}$; $\vartheta_0=\frac{uX_d-dX_u}{(1+r)(u-d)}$.
- Put–call parity: $c-p=S-\frac{K}{1+r}$; forward price $F=(1+r)S(0)$.

## A.2 Recipe — trinomial (incomplete) one-period exercise
1. Completeness: $K=3$ states, 2 assets ⇒ incomplete.
2. $Q$: write $q_2,q_3$ as functions of $q_1$ (or of the parameter asked); impose all $q_k>0$ ⇒ open interval for the parameter.
3. NA ⟺ the interval is non-empty.
4. New payoff $X$: replicable iff $\det[A|X]=0$ (3×3). If not, price $=\frac{\mathbb{E}^Q[X]}{1+r}$ is linear in the parameter ⇒ evaluate at the endpoints ⇒ open interval.
5. Super-replication $(x,y)$: write $x(1+r)+yS(1)(\omega_k)\ge X(\omega_k)$ as lines $y\ge\dots$; envelope = max of lines; minimum cost at the kink where the iso-cost slope $-1/S(0)$ fits between adjacent slopes; value = upper bound.
6. A price inside the interval ⇒ extended market NA **and complete** (rank 3) ⇒ unique $Q$ from "price = given value"; use it to price any other claim.

## A.3 Multi-period trees
- $B(t+1)=B(t)(1+r(t))$; $B(2)(\omega)=(1+r(0))(1+r(1)(f^1_h))$.
- Local $Q$ at each node: $q=\frac{S(t)(1+r(t))-S_{down}}{S_{up}-S_{down}}$; global $Q[\omega]=$ product along the path.
- NA ⟺ all local $q\in(0,1)$; complete ⟺ unique local $Q$ at every node.
- Pricing (backward): $S_X(t)=\mathbb{E}^Q_t\big[\frac{X(t+1)+S_X(t+1)}{1+r(t)}\big]$; Gordon: $S_X(t)=\mathbb{E}^Q_t\big[\sum_{\tau>t}\frac{B(t)}{B(\tau)}X(\tau)\big]$.
- ZCB: $P(0,T)=\mathbb{E}^Q[1/B(T)]$. Forward price: $F=S(0)/P(0,T)$. Futures price: $f(t)=\mathbb{E}^Q_t[S(T)]$ (no discounting). Swap rate: solve $\sum_t\mathbb{E}^Q\big[\frac{\bar r-r(t-1)}{B(t)}\big]=0$.
- American: exercise at $t$ iff immediate payoff $\ge$ continuation value.
- Hedging at a node: $\vartheta_1=\frac{V_u-V_d}{S_u-S_d}$, $\vartheta_0=\frac{V_u-\vartheta_1S_u}{B(t+1)}$.
- Path-dependent payoffs: compute along each path (max/min/average of $S(0),S(1),S(2)$).

## A.4 Stochastic calculus
- BM: $W(0)=0$, continuous, independent increments, $W(t)-W(s)\sim N(0,t-s)$.
- Itô: $d\varphi=\big(\varphi_t+\varphi_x\alpha+\frac12\varphi_{xx}\beta^2\big)dt+\varphi_x\beta\,dW$. Rules $(dW)^2=dt$, $dt\,dW=0$.
- $\mathbb{E}[e^{N(a,b^2)}]=e^{a+b^2/2}$. Itô integral: mean 0, variance $\int\mathbb{E}[\beta^2]dt$, martingale.

## A.5 Black–Scholes
- $S(t)=S_0e^{(\mu-\frac12\sigma^2)t+\sigma W^P(t)}=S_0e^{(r-\frac12\sigma^2)t+\sigma W^Q(t)}$; $W^Q=W^P+\frac{\mu-r}{\sigma}t$.
- $\mathbb{E}^P[S(t)]=S_0e^{\mu t}$, $\operatorname{Var}^P=S_0^2e^{2\mu t}(e^{\sigma^2t}-1)$; $\mathbb{E}[S^n(t)]=S_0^ne^{n\mu t+\frac12n(n-1)\sigma^2t}$ ($r$ under $Q$).
- $P[S(T)>K]=N\big(\frac{\ln(S_0/K)+(\mu-\frac12\sigma^2)T}{\sigma\sqrt T}\big)$; $Q[S(T)>K]=N(d_2)$.
- $d_{1,2}=\frac{\ln(S/K)+(r\pm\frac12\sigma^2)\tau}{\sigma\sqrt\tau}$; $c=SN(d_1)-Ke^{-r\tau}N(d_2)$; $p=Ke^{-r\tau}N(-d_2)-SN(-d_1)$; $c-p=S-Ke^{-r\tau}$.
- Digital call $e^{-r\tau}N(d_2)$; asset-or-nothing $SN(d_1)$; power $S^n$: $S^ne^{(n-1)(r+\frac n2\sigma^2)\tau}$.
- PDE: $F_t+rSF_S+\frac12\sigma^2S^2F_{SS}=rF$, $F(T,S)=f(S)$. Replication: $\vartheta_1=F_S$, $\vartheta_0=(F-F_SS)e^{-rt}$.
- Self-financing: $dV=\vartheta_0dB+\vartheta_1dS$ ⟺ $d\tilde V=\vartheta_1d\tilde S$.
- Market price of risk $\nu=\frac{\mu-r}{\sigma}$ equal for all assets driven by the same $W$.

## A.6 Recipe — Black–Scholes exercise
1. Write $S(T)=S(t)e^{(m-\frac12\sigma^2)(T-t)+\sigma\sqrt{T-t}Z}$ with $m=\mu$ ($P$) or $r$ ($Q$), $Z\sim N(0,1)$.
2. Probabilities: isolate $Z$ ⇒ $N(\cdot)$; careful with the direction ($P[Z>a]=N(-a)$).
3. Prices: decompose the payoff into known building blocks (table §16.10); otherwise compute $e^{-r\tau}\mathbb{E}^Q[\cdot]$ with the lognormal MGF.
4. At intermediate $t$: split into $\mathcal{F}_t$-measurable part (known) and the independent future increment.
5. Replication: $\vartheta_1=\partial_SF$, $\vartheta_0=e^{-rt}(F-\vartheta_1S)$; sign of $\vartheta_1$ ⇒ long/short.
6. Sensitivities: differentiate the closed form; sign via monotonicity of $N$.
